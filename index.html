about

~ a brief

I'm a PhD researcher at UCL, split across Electronic & Electrical Engineering and Science & Technology Studies, supervised by Anna Maria Mandalari. My work treats internet measurement as the technical core and STS as the interpretive layer — reading network traffic, device behaviour, and platform architecture as political objects, not just technical ones.

I'm also a Mozilla Foundation Early Career Fellow, hosted at SMEX in Beirut, and an ISOC Foundation Early Career Fellow. My fellowship work maps infrastructure dependency across the WANA region — who controls the cables, the SIMs, the satellites, and what that means when a state decides to shut a network down.

I grew up in Cairo, which is where most of this started: watching what a shutdown actually does to a city, not in theory but in practice.
