about

~ a brief

I'm a PhD researcher at UCL, split across Electronic & Electrical Engineering and Science & Technology Studies, supervised by Anna Maria Mandalari and Cian O'Donovan.

I'm currently a Mozilla Foundation Early Career Fellow, hosted at SMEX in Beirut, and an ISOC Foundation Early Career Fellow. My fellowship work maps infrastructure dependency across the SWANA region; which foreign actors have access to the data shared by civil society, and what harms this can pose.

I've been using the concept of 'counter-appropriation' (de Castro Leal et al., 2019) to describe my work: “We used to open not only the radio, but the computer, cellphones, every electronic device, to check what was inside and rebuild… Technology is a human sacrifice; every piece of technology has a drop of blood … I would like to adapt the technologies to my needs instead of myself adapting to the technology.” I am trying to use network measurements to understand the internet (and the harms it can pose) more intimately, and therefore, attempt to adapt it to the needs of specific communities and users most at-risk.
